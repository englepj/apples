    Class and sheets

    Running the game

    1. Before class. Set the class size and download the sheets. There are three kinds of eater: Taco Fan, Mixed Tastes, and Smoothie Fan, split about evenly. Each sheet lists how many points the 1st, 2nd, 3rd taco or smoothie is worth to that eater.
    2. Rules, 3 minutes. Each round you have a budget to spend at the food trucks. Buy whole tacos and smoothies, stay within budget, and score as many points as you can. Unspent money scores nothing. Work alone.
    3. Each round. Post the prices, give students about 3 minutes to choose and record their bundle and points, then tally by show of hands: "Who bought 0 tacos? 1 taco? 2?" Do the same for smoothies.
    4. Suggested order. Opening prices, then tacos at $4, then tacos at $1. That gives three points on the demand curve. Add a payday round for income effects, or the compensated round to split substitution and income effects.
    5. Debrief. Ask students to compute points per dollar for their last taco and last smoothie. Then reveal the best bundles and the demand curve on the Debrief tab.

    Tallies are saved in this browser only, so a refresh won't lose a round. Nothing about individual students is recorded.
