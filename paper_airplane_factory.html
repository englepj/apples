    Factories and costs

    The standard plane

    1. Fold a half sheet in half the long way, then open it.
    2. Fold the two top corners to the center crease.
    3. Fold the new slanted edges to the center crease again.
    4. Fold the plane in half along the crease, then fold each wing down.
    5. Draw the factory's star on both wings with the factory pen.

    A plane passes inspection if it matches the spec, has both stars, and glides past the inspector's line.

    Running the game

    1. Before class. Pick the number of factories (one per group of 6 to 9 students) and download the printable kit. Each factory gets one desk, one pen, a stack of half sheets, and a tape line on the floor about 10 feet away.
    2. Roles. In each group, one student is the inspector and one is the timekeeper. Everyone else waits to be hired.
    3. Rules, 3 minutes. Only workers on the clock may touch paper or the pen. Every plane must match the spec and carry a star on both wings. The inspector rejects anything else. Unfinished planes at the bell don't count.
    4. Rounds. Start with 1 worker per factory and run the clock. Inspectors report the planes that passed, and you type them into the production log. Clear the desk, add one worker per factory, and repeat up to about 8 workers.
    5. More capital (optional). Start a new run with 2 tables and 2 pens and repeat a few crew sizes. The cost curves for both runs appear together on the Debrief tab.
    6. Debrief. Have students compute MP, AP, MC, and ATC on the cost worksheet first. Then reveal the curves and the cost table on the Debrief tab.

    The log is saved in this browser only, so a refresh won't lose a round. Nothing about individual students is recorded.
