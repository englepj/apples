      Running the game

      1. Before class. Set the class size, deal a deck, then download the printable cards and cut them apart. Each card is one buyer (a packing house) or one seller (an orchard) with a private value or cost.
      2. Rules, 5 minutes. One crate per card per round. Buyers earn value minus price; sellers earn price minus cost. Never trade at a loss. Nobody shows their card.
      3. The pit. Everyone stands and calls out bids and asks. When a buyer and seller agree, both walk to the recorder (you or a TA) and read off their card numbers and the price. You type it in and it posts to the tape right away.
      4. Rounds. Run three open-market rounds of 4 to 5 minutes before any shock, so the class can watch prices settle. Then try a demand shift, a frost, a tax, or a price control with Next round.
      5. Shocks. Read the round headline aloud. Students adjust the number on their own card for that round (for example, buyers add $3).
      6. Debrief. Switch to the Debrief tab. Turn on Show prediction only after you've asked the class to guess where price would land.

      Trades are saved in this browser only, so a page refresh won't lose a round. Nothing about students is stored; cards are tracked by number.
